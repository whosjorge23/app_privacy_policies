Privacy Policy for Cognera

Effective Date: May 14, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Cognera ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our app.

Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account and we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number through the app.

The app stores certain gameplay and settings data locally on your device so the app can function properly. This may include:

  • Puzzle progress and completion state
  • Best times and achievement progress
  • Hint usage and in-app settings such as theme preferences

How We Use Information

Local data is used only to operate app features, including:

  • Saving your progress and unlocked content
  • Remembering settings and preferences
  • Supporting gameplay features such as hints and achievements

Advertising

Cognera may display rewarded ads to let users earn in-app hints. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When ads are loaded or shown, Google and its partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, approximate location, diagnostics, and ad interaction data in accordance with their own privacy practices.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

App Updates

The app may check whether a newer version is available through the relevant app store. This may involve contacting platform services to retrieve version information.

Camera Access

Some builds of the app may request camera permission for QR or scanning related functionality. If camera access is requested, it is used only for that feature and is not used by us to collect or store your photos or videos.

Data Storage and Security

Gameplay data and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not operate our own backend to store your puzzle progress.

While we take privacy-conscious design approaches, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Third-Party Services

The app may rely on third-party services, including:

  • Google AdMob for rewarded advertisements
  • App store or platform services for update availability checks

These third parties may process data according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Cognera is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

You can stop all collection by third-party services used in the app by uninstalling the app. You may also reset locally stored game data from within the app where that option is available.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
